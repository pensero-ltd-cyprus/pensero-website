About

Company profile

Pensero Ltd is a private limited company registered in Cyprus. The company provides consulting services to Amazon e-commerce businesses and develops and licenses software applications for e-commerce operations.

Activities

Consulting. Pensero Ltd advises businesses selling on Amazon marketplaces on product selection and development, listings, advertising, inventory and logistics, controlling and internal organisation. Consulting services

Software. Pensero Ltd has developed four web applications for inventory planning, product launches and advertising, sales reporting and customer feedback analysis. The software is licensed to business customers under licence agreements. Software applications

Management

Pensero Ltd was founded by Eduard Denk, who is the company’s director. Before founding Pensero Ltd, he was Chief Operating Officer of an Amazon business, with responsibility for operations, sales growth and profitability over a period of three and a half years. He directs the company’s consulting activities and the development of its software applications.

Susanne Denk is responsible for back-office operations.

Working languages: English, German and Russian.
